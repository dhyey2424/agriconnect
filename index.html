Questions

Everything you need to understand the platform.

What makes AgriConnect different? +
It combines market intelligence, AI-assisted visual assessment, verified marketplace listings and competitive bidding into one decision journey rather than treating them as separate tools.
Are the prices shown real? +
No. This prototype clearly labels its market, quality and bid values as illustrative demo data. A production system would connect to validated market data sources.
Is the AI assessment a certification? +
No. It is a preliminary visual signal based on observable image characteristics. It cannot verify internal quality, pesticide residue, moisture, taste or nutrition.
Can farmers compare buyers? +
Yes. The prototype includes a bid comparison workflow designed to compare gross price alongside logistics and estimated net realization.
Can buyers inspect individual produce listings? +
Yes. Buyers can move from the marketplace to listing detail, inspect available information and then participate in the bidding journey.

01 · The idea

A marketplace built around the farmer's decision.

Instead of stopping at a listing and a highest bid, AgriConnect connects the pieces that matter before a deal is accepted.

01 / KNOW

Know your price.

See an indicative market reference and suggested listing range before you publish.

02 / ASSESS

See your produce.

Use AI-assisted visual assessment to generate a preliminary A/B/C grade from observable photo characteristics.

03 / CONNECT

Let buyers compete.

Verified bulk buyers compete on listings while the farmer compares the outcome that matters.
